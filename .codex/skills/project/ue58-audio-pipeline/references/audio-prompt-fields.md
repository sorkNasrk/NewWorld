# Audio Prompt Fields

## SFX And UI

Include trigger, physical source, duration, transient shape, attack/decay/tail, material character, frequency range, distance, 2D/3D behavior, variation count, forbidden traits, and UE routing.

## Music And Ambient

Include purpose, gameplay state, loop length, BPM, meter, rhythm density, instrumentation, harmonic complexity, energy curve, stem/layer needs, mix position, transition points, forbidden references, and loop review.

## Voice

Include exact text, language, speaker profile, emotion, intent, pace, pauses, pronunciation, nonverbal sounds, subtitle key, file naming, localization plan, and forbidden traits.

## QA

Check loudness and peaks, audible clicks/pops, loop seams, fatigue after repeated playback, spatialization, attenuation, and whether the asset supports gameplay readability.
